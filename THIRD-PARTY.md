## Fonts

All three fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Each licence text sits next to its font in `assets/fonts`.

| Font | Used for | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jacquard 24 | Titles, Warden names | [OFL-Jacquard24.txt](assets/fonts/OFL-Jacquard24.txt) |
| Pixelify Sans | Interface, dialogue | [OFL-PixelifySans.txt](assets/fonts/OFL-PixelifySans.txt) |
| Jersey 10 | Numbers, keys, labels | [OFL-Jersey10.txt](assets/fonts/OFL-Jersey10.txt) |

## Art

Every sprite, tile, icon and the mark are drawn for this game as text grids in `tools/art` and rendered by `tools/art/build.py`. They are covered by the project's MIT licence.
